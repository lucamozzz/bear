The collaboration involves three participants: a Customer, A bike Rental and an Insurer 
The collaboration begins when the client sends a rental request through the app. Upon receiving the request, the bike rental system identifies the rental point closest to the client’s current location and checks for the availability of a bicycle. If no bikes are available at the selected station, the system automatically searches for the next nearest rental point and repeats the availability check. Once a bike is found, the system immediately sends the client the coordinates of the rental point where the bicycle is available.
Before going to the rental location, the client is prompted to choose whether or not to purchase insurance for the ride. If the client opts for insurance, the bike rental service communicates with the insurer to activate the policy and, once confirmed, sends the client the unlock code required to access the bicycle. If the client declines insurance, the system bypasses the insurer and directly provides the unlock code.
The client then travels to the rental point, unlocks the bicycle using the code, and proceeds with the ride. After reaching the destination, the client takes a photo of the bicycle to document its condition at the end of the rental and submits it through the app. Payment is then processed, and the client waits for feedback from the bike rental service.
Once the photo has been received, the bike rental team reviews it to assess the condition of the bicycle. If no damage is detected, the client receives a positive feedback message. If any issues are identified, the bike rental service evaluates whether the client had opted for insurance. Depending on the insurance status, either the insurer or the client is contacted to cover the damage costs or pay the applicable fine.
