Description: The collaboration involves two participants: a controller and a planter robot called planter for semplicity. 

The controller initiates the collaboration by activating the planter. Once activated, the planter remains idle, waiting for the controller to provide information about the available areas where tomato seedlings can be planted.
As soon as the planter receives the planting area details, it moves to the designated location, plants the tomato crop, and sends a notification indicating that the task has been completed.
Upon receiving the completion notification, the controller confirms that the planting task is finished and informs the planter accordingly. The planter then returns to its base and stop its engine.
