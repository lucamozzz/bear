Description: The collaboration involves three participants: a controller a sensor and a sprinkler robot. 
In this scenario a sensor monitors the humidity level of the ground. If the humidity level of the ground is found to be insufficient, the sensor sends a notification to the controller.
Upon receiving the alert, the controller identifies the affected area and sends the location details to the sprinkler robot. The sprinkler then moves to the specified area, irrigates the soil, and sends a confirmation notification to the controller upon successful completion of the task.
After finishing the irrigation, the sprinkler returns to its base, ready for future operations.
