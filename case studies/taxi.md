The collaboration involves three participants: Person 1, Person 2, and a Taxi Driver. Two friends (Person 1 and Person2), each located at their own home, plan to go to the cinema together. Person 1 books a taxi and asks the driver to first pick them up and then stop by Person 2’s home as well. The taxi arrives at Person 1’s location, picks them up, and then proceeds to Person 2’s home to pick up the second passenger. Once both are on board, the taxi heads to the cinema. At the destination, the taxi driver informs Person 1—the one who made the booking—of the total fare. After payment, both friends exit the taxi. 
The scenario takes place in an urban environment, which includes a street, several buildings, the homes of the two friends, and the cinema as the final destination.
